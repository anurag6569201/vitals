Questions

Does Vitals slow my Mac down or drain the battery itself?

No. It checks every 5 seconds (every 10 while you're away) and typically uses well under 1% CPU. Watching it in Activity Monitor is encouraged.

Will it nag me?

Vitals only speaks up when a problem has lasted long enough to matter. Pick Relaxed, Balanced or Sensitive, snooze any alert, or tell it to always ignore an app.

What does Vitals send over the internet?

Nothing about your Mac. The only requests are activating your license key and a daily check for a new version.

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

Apple's App Store sandbox doesn't let apps see which app is using your CPU or battery, or quit it for you. Those are the whole point of Vitals, so the full version is a direct download, notarized by Apple.

Which Macs are supported?

Any Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Battery features need a MacBook.
