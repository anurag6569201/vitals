Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Vitals is a Mac app that monitors your Mac's health. It is designed to keep everything on your Mac.

What Vitals reads

CPU, memory, disk, network totals, battery and thermal state, the names of running apps and processes, and which apps are preventing sleep. This is used only to show readings and alerts on your Mac.

What Vitals stores

Your settings, a short history of readings (about 20 minutes, in memory) and your recent "While you were away" reports, stored locally in the app's preferences. Nothing is uploaded.

What Vitals sends

  • License activation: if you enter a license key, the key and your Mac's name are sent to Lemon Squeezy (our payment provider) to activate and occasionally validate it.
  • Update check: once a day Vitals downloads a small file describing the latest version. No identifiers are sent.

Vitals contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not sell or share any data.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as merchant of record. See their privacy policy for how they process payment information.
