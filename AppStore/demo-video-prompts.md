# Vitals — demo video with Veo

The App Store doesn't need a video, so this one is optional. It's useful for the website hero, X/Reddit posts and a Product Hunt launch.

**How to use:** pick image-to-video and give Veo the listed screenshot (from `AppStore/screenshots/`) as the **first frame**, so the real UI stays sharp. Video models garble small UI text, so keep the camera moves gentle and don't ask Veo to *create* app screens. Keep each clip at 6–8 s, 16:9, 1080p. Cut the clips together in iMovie/CapCut and add a soft synth bed and on-screen captions there; the captions are listed per shot.

---

### Shot 1 — Hook (no screenshot, text-to-video)
> Cinematic close-up of a modern silver laptop on a wooden desk at golden hour, screen glowing softly, a small orange warning light pulses once in the top-right corner of the screen like a car's check-engine light, then calms to a gentle green. Shallow depth of field, slow push-in, warm natural light, minimal, Apple-style product film. No text, no logos.

Caption: **Your Mac has a check-engine light now.**

### Shot 2 — The popover (first frame: `01-alerts.png`)
> Slow, smooth dolly-in toward the right-hand panel of this image, subtle parallax, soft light bloom on the orange alert icon, the background gradient drifts gently. Keep all interface elements perfectly still and legible. No new text.

Caption: **Quiet when everything's fine. Clear when it isn't.**

### Shot 3 — Free Up Space (first frame: `02-free-up-space.png`)
> Gentle camera glide from left to right across this app window, slight 3D tilt, soft shadow beneath the window, background glow pulses slowly. Interface stays sharp and unchanged.

Caption: **Find 13 GB of developer junk in seconds.**

### Shot 4 — Space Map (first frame: `03-space-map.png`)
> Slow zoom into the colorful treemap blocks in the center of the window, the blocks catch a subtle light sweep from left to right, calm and precise. UI remains static and readable.

Caption: **See exactly where your space went.**

### Shot 5 — Battery (first frame: `04-battery.png`)
> Slow pull-back from the battery ring at the top-left of the window, a soft green glow breathes around the ring, gentle floating motion. No changes to text or numbers.

Caption: **Battery health, cycles and "lasts until…"**

### Shot 6 — Pin (first frame: `05-pin.png`)
> Subtle camera drift toward the small dark dashboard widget on the left, its bars shimmer lightly as if live, background gradient flows slowly. Interface stays crisp.

Caption: **Pin your vitals anywhere.**

### Shot 7 — End card (text-to-video, or a still)
> Minimal dark navy gradient background with a soft blue glow rising from the bottom, a small pulse-line icon in a rounded blue square fades in at the center, calm, premium, Apple-style. Leave space below for text.

Overlay in your editor: **Vitals — Free on the Mac App Store. Pro $9.99 once.** and **vitalsformac.com**

---

**Tip:** for a version you can trust 100%, screen-record the real app with QuickTime (File › New Screen Recording), with the popover opening, Free Up Space scanning, and the pin switching presets. Use the Veo shots only for the hook (1) and end card (7).
